Experimental Features

Signing in to Linux apps

Why a sign-in page opens in Linux when every other link opens on your Mac.

Links from Linux apps open on your Mac - that is the point of the integration. Sign-in pages are the one exception, and they have to be.

Why the Mac cannot finish a sign-in#

A "sign in with..." flow ends by handing its answer back to the machine that started it, in one of two ways:

How it comes backWhat it means
To 127.0.0.1 on some portThe Linux app is listening there. Your Mac is not.
To a private address like discord://Only Linux knows which app that belongs to.

Open that page on your Mac and the answer arrives on your Mac: the browser either shows an error, or silently hands it to a Mac app of the same name. The Linux app never hears anything and waits forever.

So the sign-in page - and only that page - opens in the instance's own browser, where the whole round trip stays on one machine.

What you will see#

  1. You press "Sign in" in a Linux app.
  2. A browser window opens inside Linux, not on your Mac.
  3. You sign in there.
  4. The app picks up where it left off.

The one cost is that this browser is not your Mac's, so it has none of your saved passwords. It is usually a one-time step per app.

What still opens on your Mac#

Everything else. MSL decides by reading the link, and only diverts one that carries the marks of an authorisation request - a sign-in address to return to, or an app identifier with a response type. An ordinary link, a documentation page, a search result, a link in an email: all still open in your own browser.

note

It needs a browser in the instance

With no browser installed in Linux there is nothing to open the page in, so the link goes to your Mac as before. That will not complete the sign-in, but a page that opens somewhere beats a page that opens nowhere.

If it gets one wrong#

This is new, and it judges by reading the link. If an ordinary page starts opening in the guest's browser, turn off Sign-in pages open in Linux in Experimental Features - every link then goes to your Mac again, and sign-ins go back to not completing.
