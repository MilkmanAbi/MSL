The Main Window

Instance logos

Give an instance its own badge — a symbol and colour, or a picture of your own.

MSL draws every instance as its distro's symbol in its distro's colour. That is right until you have four Debians — then the sidebar is four identical rows and the name is the only thing telling them apart.

Click an instance's badge — in the sidebar, or the big one in the instance header — and pick something else. Change Logo… in the right-click menu opens the same panel.

What you can pick#

FactionSixty symbols in five groups — Creatures, Banners, Work, Places and Marks. Click the one you've already chosen to clear it.
ColourTwelve colours. With none chosen, the badge keeps the distro's own.
Open…A picture of your own, from anywhere on your Mac.

Pictures#

Open… takes any image your Mac can read. MSL copies it in rather than remembering where it was, so moving, renaming or deleting the original later doesn't leave you with a blank badge. The copy is squared off from the middle and stored small — a badge is 44 points at most, so there's no point keeping a twelve-megapixel photo for it.

A picture covers the symbol, so the faction grid dims while one is showing. Remove Picture brings the symbol back without losing the faction and colour you chose.

Reset to Default#

At the top, beside Open…. It drops everything — symbol, colour and picture — and the instance goes back to looking like its distro.

note

It's only on your Mac

The logo is MSL's own note about the instance, not something inside Linux. It isn't in snapshots, it doesn't travel in a shipment, and removing the instance forgets it — so a new instance that reuses the name starts plain rather than inheriting a badge.
